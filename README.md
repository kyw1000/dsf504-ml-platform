The dsf504-ml-platform is the sample Dashboard and sample use cases for the machine learning platform taught in DSF504 Practice of Big Data and Analysis for Finance.
This is a demo for the final project for the students.
The use cases covered are as follows:
DSF504 The Practice of Big Data and Analytics in Finance, Spring 2025
AI/ML Use Cases in Finance: 
Domains, Problems, and Value
A structured project selection and executive discussion

1. Financial Crime & Fraud Analytics
Included Use Cases:
•	Credit Card Fraud Detection
•	Fraud / Anomaly Detection
•	Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Detection

Key Problems:
•	Extremely imbalanced data (fraud < 0.1%)
•	High false positives leading to customer friction
•	Evolving fraud patterns and adversarial behavior
•	Regulatory compliance requirements (FATF, Basel guidelines)

ML Value:
•	Detection of complex behavioral anomalies (Bolton & Hand, 2002)
•	Real-time transaction risk scoring (Bhattacharyya et al., 2011)
•	Adaptive learning vs rule-based systems
•	Graph/network analytics for AML (Weber et al., 2019)

Business Impact:
•	Reduced fraud losses
•	Lower investigation costs
•	Improved compliance outcomes
•	Enhanced customer trust
2. Credit Risk & Default Intelligence
Included Use Cases:
•	Loan Default Prediction / Credit Scoring
•	Early Warning Signals for Loans or Investments

Key Problems:
•	Incomplete borrower information
•	Lagging indicators in traditional models
•	Macroeconomic sensitivity
•	Regulatory requirement for explainability (Basel II/III)

ML Value:
•	Nonlinear risk modeling (Lessmann et al., 2015)
•	Integration of alternative data sources (Bazarbash, 2019)
•	Early deterioration detection
•	Improved borrower segmentation

Business Impact:
•	Reduced default rates
•	Improved credit approval precision
•	Higher risk-adjusted returns
•	Optimized capital allocation
3. Investment & Market Intelligence
Included Use Cases:
•	Stock Return / Volatility Prediction
•	Crypto Price Movement Prediction
•	Financial News Sentiment Analysis
•	Portfolio Rebalancing and Risk Monitoring

Key Problems:
•	High noise and market randomness
•	Non-stationary time series
•	Information overload
•	Difficulty linking signals to decisions

ML Value:
•	Signal extraction from noisy data (Fama, 1970; Gu et al., 2020)
•	NLP-based sentiment analysis (Tetlock, 2007)
•	Dynamic risk modeling
•	Pattern recognition across assets

Business Impact:
•	Improved alpha generation
•	Enhanced risk-adjusted returns
•	Faster response to market events
•	Better portfolio allocation decisions
4. Customer Intelligence & Retention
Included Use Cases:
•	Customer Churn Prediction

Key Problems:
•	Silent churn without explicit signals
•	High acquisition cost vs retention
•	Fragmented customer data
•	Limited behavioral visibility

ML Value:
•	Predictive churn modeling (Verbeke et al., 2012)
•	Customer segmentation and targeting
•	Behavioral pattern detection
•	Personalized retention strategies

Business Impact:
•	Increased customer lifetime value
•	Reduced churn rate
•	Improved cross-selling
•	Higher engagement
5. Insurance Risk & Claims Analytics
Included Use Cases:
•	Insurance Claim Risk Prediction

Key Problems:
•	Fraudulent claims
•	Information asymmetry
•	Complex risk profiles
•	Pricing inefficiencies

ML Value:
•	Fraud detection in claims (Viaene et al., 2002)
•	Risk-based pricing models
•	Pattern recognition in claims data
•	Automated underwriting

Business Impact:
•	Reduced claim losses
•	Improved underwriting accuracy
•	Operational efficiency
•	Increased profitability
6. ESG, Compliance & Reputation Risk
Included Use Cases:
•	ESG / Greenwashing Risk Scoring

Key Problems:
•	Lack of standardized ESG data
•	Greenwashing risk
•	Regulatory pressure
•	Inconsistent disclosures

ML Value:
•	NLP analysis of ESG reports (Berg et al., 2022)
•	Cross-source validation
•	Anomaly detection in disclosures
•	Automated ESG scoring

Business Impact:
•	Reduced reputational risk
•	Improved ESG credibility
•	Better sustainable investment decisions
7. Intelligent Advisory & Decision Support
Included Use Cases:
•	Robo-Advisory Portfolio Recommendation
•	Explainable AI for Analysts & Managers

Key Problems:
•	Limited scalability of human advisory
•	Black-box model concerns
•	Complex decision environments
•	Regulatory explainability requirements

ML Value:
•	Automated portfolio optimization (Markowitz, 1952)
•	Personalized recommendations
•	Explainable AI methods (Ribeiro et al., 2016; Lundberg & Lee, 2017)
•	Human-AI collaborative decision making

Business Impact:
•	Scalable advisory services
•	Improved client trust
•	Faster and better decisions
•	Reduced reliance on manual analysis
